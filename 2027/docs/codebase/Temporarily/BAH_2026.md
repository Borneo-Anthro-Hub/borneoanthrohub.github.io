### Section 4 — FAQ (Ticket)

**Q1:** How do I purchase a BAH Entry Ticket?

```text
To secure yourself a slot, select your preferred tier and you will be redirected to fill in the Ticketing Form. All the information you need to know will be available in the form itself. Please refer to the BAH Guide for detailed information.
```

**Q2:** Do I get a confirmation when my registration has gone through?

```text
Yes! Once your submission has been verified, you will receive a Confirmation Email from us within 72 hours, alongside with the link to your Convention Profile Form.
A copy of your response will also sent to you. Please keep that copy for reference.
Should you have not received your Confirmation Email after the timeframe, please inform the committee for further inquiries.
```

**Q3:** Are BAH Ticket Upgrades Available?

```text
Yes, and its available for a limited time only with limited slots!

- Only limited to registered BAHpals - A (Attendees)
- The Ticket Upgrade Google Form Link can be found here: https://forms.gle/ZBsuPfXYsGz4shCS8
- Please retrieve your existing Ticket ID from your previous confirmation email, and fill in the form as required.
- Should you have any issues, or need us to re-send the previous confirmation email for your Ticket ID, you may write in to us at our Email - admin@borneoanthrohub.com and we'll address it as soon as possible.
```

**Q4:** Can I customize my Con-Badge?

```text
Yes! Feel free to update your information accordingly in the Convention Profile Form, which you can find the link to it in your confirmation email.
Please note that certain options is only applicable for Sponsors and Super Sponsors only.
Please also be mindful of the cut-off date (3 months before the convention) for you to update your information.
```

**Q5:** What is the Age Eligibility to participate in the convention?

```text
Participation is only open to individuals aged 16 and above with a valid ticket.

- Individuals aged 16 or 17 must present a signed Parental Consent Form during collection at the convention.
- The Parental / Guardian Consent Form can be found here: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NEuAK-RdGpIZhsJNacY4daNYfuBG66iS/view?usp=sharing
- Please print it and have it signed by a parent or guardian.
- Bring the signed form to the convention and submit it at the registration counter during check-in.
```

**Q6:** I am 17 years old when booking the ticket, but I will be 18 when attending the convention. Do I still need to submit the Parental / Guardian Consent Form?

```text
If you are 18 during the time of the convention, it is not compulsory to provide the parental consent form, but it is still advisable to provide one for safety reasons.
```

**Q7:** Are the tickets refundable or transferable?

```text
Tickets sold are non-refundable. However, transfers are available under these conditions:

- You must inform us of the transfer by writing in to us.
- Transfers must be done before the cut-off date (3 months before the convention).
  The responsibility of the transfer will be solely between the "Transferrer" and "Transferee".
```

**Q8:** In the event that I am unable to attend due to unforeseen consequences, can I assign someone to collect my items on behalf?

```text
Yes, please ensure you write in to us of your unavailability with the details of the Attendee (Ticket Number and Attendee Information) that will be collecting on your behalf to our email - admin@borneoanthrohub.com. On collection day, you may show us the acknowledgement reply and we'll handle the arranged collection.
```

### Section 4 — FAQ (Join Us)

#### Panelist FAQ

**1. Who can register as a Panelist?**

```text
Anyone may do so! We also welcome any topics to be discussed, as long as you have a valid admission ticket for BAH 2026. A Panelist tag/sticker (or in the form of other objects of identification) will also be given to you on the respective Collection Day.
```

**2. Does submitting a form guarantee a panel spot?**

```text
No. Panel submissions are manually reviewed and selected after discussion, no guarantees are promised. Accepted panelists will be notified by email, subject to schedule and venue availability. Please provide sufficient or detailed information which would help us get to know more about your topic and help in the manual review process.
```

**3. Do I get to schedule or pick a venue for my panel?**

```text
Changes may be discussed, but all adjustments (time and venues) will be determined based on our availability to cater the proposed changes (if available).
```

**4. What are my responsibilities as a Panelist?**

```text
As a panelist, you are responsible for:
- Providing accurate and truthful information.
- Ensuring panel content is lawful and respectful.
- Managing panel attendees fairly, ethically and without discriminations.
```

**5. Can I collect personal data during my panel?**

```text
Yes, but not recommended. Should you choose to do so, you must ensure full compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA 2010) and the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024. As long as proper notice and consent is provided from attendees, you may collect data for lawful purposes, and never to misuse or sell nor retaining data longer than necessary.
Any updates to Malaysian privacy laws will be in accordance with official announcements.
Borneo Anthro Hub assumes no legal liability for your data collection activities.
```

**6. Who owns the content created for a panel?**

```text
You will retain FULL ownership of your content, but grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to host, store and display the content for archival or operational purposes.
```

**7. Will BAH be responsible for disputes, losses or claims related to my panel?**

```text
No. BAH will not be responsible nor liable for any disputes, misuse, indirect or consequential claims or damages arising from panel activities or breaching of rules. In the event that your panel violates any rules, BAH reserves the right to suspend your panel or restrict panelist access without prior notice.
```

**8. Got concerns that are not covered?**

```text
You may write in to us at our Email - admin@borneoanthrohub.com and we'll address it as soon as possible.
```

#### Volunteer FAQ

**1. Who can apply as a Volunteer?**

```text
Any valid attendee of any tier (BAHpals A/S/SS) who wishes to lend a helping hand may register as a Volunteer for the convention. A separate instruction will be given to you to collect your identification.
```

**Entry/Visa Requirements:**

```text
- West Malaysians / Locals: No visa needed. MyKad required. Journey/Visit Pass Slip issued for up to 90 days.
- Foreign Nationals: Check eligibility at https://embassymalaysia.nl/list-of-countries-that-need-a-visa-to-enter-malaysia/
```

```text
1. All dutiable/prohibited goods must be declared
2. Pork products NOT allowed in/out of state
3. Fresh produce NOT allowed into state
4. Cannot bring RM exceeding USD 10,000 cash without BNM approval
5. Penalties: up to RM3 million fine or 5 years imprisonment
```

```text
- KKIA (BKI) — Kota Kinabalu International Airport
- GMT+8 Timezone
```
