Restaurants

If you are a BAHpal flying into Sabah from somewhere else, you would find yourself landing at Terminal 1 of KKIA (Kota Kinabalu International Airport) as your starting point.

From there, you will have several transport options to navigate towards Horizon Hotel, including Airport Taxis, local e-Hailing services or pre-booked services.

E-hailing

Using e-hailing services is one of the main ways to get around here. The Grab or AirAsia MOVE Apps are some of the more popular services widely used within Kota Kinabalu.

You may use KFC KKIA (T1) or the Airport Cafe KKIA as nearby pickup stations as there are no enforced designated waiting stations in KKIA.

Subsequently, you may walk to Arrival Hall 3 or Pillar No. 5 for pickups via Grab. Please follow the instructions as provided by the Grab App.

Airport Taxis

Another option is to book a taxi at the airport. These taxis are operated officially by the airport, so just look for the designated counter, pay upfront (fixed price) and receive one based on their available schedule.


Fares are fixed and guaranteed, but subject to traffic conditions and availability.



If you are approached by individuals offering taxi or private car services, do not engage with them as they might be unlicensed and scam operators.

Driving

Driving to Horizon Hotel is fairly straightforward, but if it's your first time navigating around Kota Kinabalu, we recommend using your favourite trustworthy navigation app to find your way there. While parking at the hotel shouldn't be a huge issue, you may need to take note that only cashless payment methods are accepted there.

Be sure to always check and make sure that you have enough fuel before setting off!
