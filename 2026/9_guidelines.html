Guidelines



1. Always wear your designated badge within con spaces.

2. If you feel unwell, please quarantine, wear a mask, and maintain distance.

3. Dress appropriately. Explicit or indecent attire is not permitted.

4. Smoking or vaping is not permitted within con spaces or hotel premises.

5. Imitation weaponry or props must be approved by the Security Team.

6. Contraband or being under the influence may result in ejection.

7. Avoid vandalizing hotel property. Damage will be held accountable.

8. No photography or videography within the Fursuit Lounge.

9. Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.

10. Seek assistance at the Con Ops Room for support or inquiries.
